How do you run a Boba Bash?
Body, Poppins

Boba Bash is a workshop based coding meetup for high schoolers, ran simultaneously in 20+ cities. For attending the workshop, you'll all receive free boba.

Components

What is a Boba Bash?

apply!

tell us your city and roughly what you're planning.

plan

you get an event page, a handbook, and a checklist.

event!

everyone shows up, builds something small, drinks boba.

Texture

a light checkerboard pattern sits behind the "how it works" and FAQ sections, keeps things feeling handmade instead of flat.

Voice

"i can't lie i have no idea"

lowercase, casual, a little self-deprecating. short sentences. explain the actual thing plainly: "you show up, get boba, build something small for a few hours."
